## Learned User Preferences
- Deliver real Microsoft Word documents for protocol/docs work; do not leave zero-byte placeholders.
- In the literature matrix, leave Period, Data Used, Method, and Key Findings blank (or marked pending) until systematic full-text extraction; do not invent those fields from title/abstract alone, and do not guess sensors or datasets (e.g. Landsat) without full text.
- Do not mark World Bank Policy Research Working Papers as Peer Reviewed = Yes; record them as not confirmed / working paper.
- Follow a seed-by-seed Litmaps workflow: add only the instructed paper and tags; do not Explore Related, save grey suggested nodes, download PDFs, or finalize research gaps until asked; record blue-outline recommendation nodes as C### candidates first and do not Add to Litmap until after screening; save verification screenshots under `02_Literature_Review/Litmaps_Screenshots/` using step-based names like `litmaps_step_2_2_*_v01.png`; when the user pastes Litmaps screenshots, treat those as verification evidence.
- Match the user’s specified completion-report format, and keep Research_Progress_Log Status / Problem/Issue fields synced when the user asks to edit the report; when asked for a simple summary (e.g. “sohoje bolo”), explain what was done in plain Bangla before dense technical detail.
- Prefer final journal issue / version-of-record year over online-first, copyright-line, or Litmaps node year; record Online first separately in notes; use DOI as the primary unique identifier and do not create duplicate matrix rows for Litmaps author/year label mismatches.
- When applying paper completion packages, never replace the master `Literature_Review_Matrix.xlsx` with a generated matrix; merge only that paper’s completed fields into the existing workbook.
- For paywalled papers without full text, keep them as partial reviews from verified abstract and publisher snippets rather than excluding them; when a publisher binary PDF is blocked but author-uploaded or other open full text exists, complete the review from that text (status Full-text reviewed) and note the full-text source. Prefer PDFs in priority order: official publisher Version of Record, then institutional-repository final published version, then ResearchGate/author-uploaded copy after metadata verification; never save a placeholder or tiny 2–3 KB working file as a PDF (record "Full PDF not downloaded — subscription access" instead); name saved PDFs with the final citation year, not the online-first/preprint year.
- When same authors share a final publication year, use APA same-year lettering (e.g. 2023a/2023b) by reference-title alphabetical order; keep overlapping same-author/same-design papers as separate matrix rows but treat them as one related evidence family in synthesis without auto-copying fields or double-counting findings.
- Keep causal estimates, scenario/ML predictions, mechanism hypotheses, and conservation indicators as separate evidence layers; treat early descriptive forest-loss counts (e.g. 2017 Hansen AOI loss) as descriptive signals only until SDID/causal analysis is complete; treat ecosystem-service (ESV) / benefit-transfer results as supplementary to core forest-loss, fragmentation, and above-ground carbon outcomes; keep distinct spatial domains explicit and do not compare them as if they share the same denominator; treat wildlife-corridor / elephant-route / habitat-connectivity findings as supplementary conservation-priority layers, not primary causal outcomes; do not infer species movement or causation from spatial overlap alone; do not use ambiguous casualty/mortality figures without primary-source verification; record authors' policy recommendations (e.g. repatriation) as their position rather than adopted conclusions; and avoid language that collectively blames the refugee population.
- When updating the full research report, copy forward to a new stage-named version under `01_Report/` (e.g. v05 Boundary Stage Frozen → v06 Annual Exposure Reconstruction); never delete or overwrite prior report versions.
- Never edit or overwrite raw official camp-boundary source files; store processed and derived layers separately. For HDX/official boundary inventories, record both Dataset_Page and Resource_Page (resource-specific links, not only the generic dataset page).
